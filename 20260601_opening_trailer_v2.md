## The shift

v2.1 is a **season premiere** — it opens on the brand logo, then "Eight Doubles. One village. One survives." That tells a stranger *the rules of a show* before they have any reason to care.

v2 is a **sales pitch for Doubland that wraps the season trailer**. We do **not** rebuild the engine. We keep everything that works — cast intros, Survival format, drama, vote dread, the live CTA — and we **bolt a Doubland concept intro on the front (A + B + C) and a main-character turn on the back (D)**.

The arc a cold viewer now travels:

> **Ache** ("what if I could…") → **Promise** ("you can — meet your Double") → **Proof** (the Pistsov season, alive) → **Participation** ("what if yours was next?").

The trailer gets ~25–30s longer. That's accepted — the front-loaded concept is what turns a season promo into a product pitch.

## The new beats — final copy + direction

### A — Hook (0:00–0:09)

> **Open on a face, not a logo.** The single most important fix in this whole pass. The first 3 seconds must be an intimate, charged moment of a Double mid-life — never the wordmark, never black.

**VO (locked):**
> "What if you had a second chance to make it right?"
> "What if you could practice that hard conversation beforehand?"
> "What if you had a Double?"

**Visual, line by line:**
- L1 → a Double pausing at a threshold — the turn-back, the second-guess.
- L2 → a charged one-on-one (kitchen table, leaning in, mid-sentence).
- L3 → reveal: pull back to show the Double living in the village — *or* a person watching their Double on a screen.

*Producer's note — optional tighter cut (not locked):* "What if you could go back and say it? / What if you could practice — before it's real? / What if you had a Double?" Terser = punchier for sound-off social. Your call.

### B — Concept (0:09–0:26)

**VO (locked):**
> "You can. In Doubland.
> Create your Double — your personality twin. Same voice, same instincts.
> Then watch it live the life you didn't.
> It's your show. And you're the main character."

**Visual:** the Double moving through Doubland, *living* — small autonomous choices, a real moment with another Double. Optional split/match of person ↔ Double to sell "twin."

**Why this resolves the earlier contradiction:** the Double is **you** (recognizable — voice, instincts), living a life you **didn't** (the road not taken). We never call it "your own life." "It's your show, you're the main character" is the emotional payoff *and* it plants the word that the close (D) pays off.

### Logo (0:26–0:32)

`brand_opener_motion.mp4` (6.04s wordmark push-in) **relocates from Stage 0 to here** — it lands as the *button on the promise*, the answer to the hook, instead of a silent cold open. This is what frees A to open on a face.

### C — Season bridge (0:32–0:40)

**On-screen / VO (locked):**
> "The Pistsov family. This season, their Doubles in Survival mode."

**Visual (show, don't tell):** the **real Pistsov family** (faces / sketches) → smash-cut to **their Doubles in the village**. Let the image carry "they're alive"; the line only names *who* and *what season*.

Then hand **straight into the existing format-lock copy** ("In real life, they're family — here, their Doubles have one rule: survive each other. Every day, a challenge. Every night, a vote."). C replaces the *old cold open's* concept job; the format lock is kept verbatim.

*Producer's note:* "in Survival mode" reads very slightly software-y ("mode" is a product word). If you ever want it to feel more like a real stakes-challenge than a sim setting, "**their Doubles take on Survival**" or "**enter Survival**" does it. Keeping your "Survival mode" as the locked line for now.

### D — Main-character turn (~2:15, just before the CTA)

**VO (locked):**
> "You've been watching someone else's Double.
> What if yours was next?"

**Visual:** pull off the season's faces to a held, waiting beat — an empty chair / a "your Double here" space / the village at rest. The turn from spectator to *protagonist-in-waiting*.

This is the callback to B: B said *you're* the main character; the whole middle showed someone else being it; D asks the viewer to claim it.

```
…vote dread (kept)
```

## What this means for the pipeline (high level — not a code spec)

- **New narration beats** (A hook, B concept, C bridge, D turn): four new Tier-B prompts + cache keys in the showrunner, same pattern as the existing 6 Burnett beats. Cohort-agnostic except C and D (which name the cohort).
- **Compose re-order:** brand-logo stage moves from Stage 0 to after B; A/B/C prepend; D inserts before the end card. No new asset *commissions* — A/C reuse existing sim captures + hero/family stills; the logo motion already exists.
- **Retired on-screen text:** keep "Who will stay alive?" off the card (already retired in v2.1 in favor of bare "What if?"); carry the season through the word **"Survival."**

Everything else rides on the v2.1 engine unchanged.
