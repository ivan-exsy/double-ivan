**Last updated:** 2026-10-15
**Status:** Recommendation. Not founder-locked. Not eng SOT. Nothing here has been sold.

## How we make money

Friend groups play free, with their Showrunner as an unpaid partner. A company pays for a private season for its team. Anyone already in a running season can buy a one-time pack of their own Double's cuts. Nothing has been sold.

Say that sentence in a seed room, plus: cost scales with Doubles, not with viewers. Do not say that people will pay for a daily film, and do not quote a willingness-to-pay number, until a group outside LeaderTalks has paid.

One paid Team Season can carry many free friend seasons. The Season Cut cannot. That is why the company sale comes first.

## What is free, what is sold, what stays unbuilt

**Free**

- Watching LeaderTalks, being in the cast, and claiming a Double.
- A friend-group season. Members do not pay. Showrunners do not pay.
- One group episode per night (about 60–120 seconds).
- One free personal spotlight per member, once spotlights exist. Everyone gets one before anyone gets a second. A member may decline. No humiliation cut. A voted-out member may get a private farewell.

**First sale — Team Season**

- A private premiere-plus-15-night season, up to 15 Doubles, on a page only that team can open.
- Ask $2,500. Floor $2,000. Do not show $299. No pilot discount.
- Perks: title card, a town set at their office and local spots, a choice of ruleset, every member's Season Cut included, premiere and finale cuts, a finale reel, kickoff and wrap calls, invoice or card.
- The team lead may pick a spotlight. The team lead does not read diaries, quiz answers, or private talk.
- Dignity exits ("moves on"). Opt-in. Employer never sees quiz results. Deletion on request. No HR or performance use. These are product rules, written on the page, not only in a terms doc.
- Founder runs the first seasons. A paid town does not run unattended.
- Cap early team work at 2–3 seasons, and never take a friend group's sim slot. No SSO, no custom build.

**Second sale — Season Cut**

- $9–15 once, for the next 5–7 personal cuts. Not a subscription. Not a daily film plan.
- One button, immediately after that person's own free spotlight, naming the moment ("your Double's betrayal on night 9").
- Wallet pay. No countdown. The group episode never contains the offer. Never show who bought.
- Sold from group two on, and only after one personal cut has been rendered and timed.
- If fewer than about 10% of spotlighted members buy, change what the offer points at before changing the price.
- Gifts come after a person can buy a cut for themselves.

**Do not build for six months**

- A solo subscription, or solos joining a featured / master cast.
- A stranger town.
- A daily-film subscription, a viewer membership, or a scenario store.
- A usage meter on the landing page, ads, or paid acquisition.
- Self-serve corporate checkout before two real buyers have asked how to pay.
- Cash, stipend, or revenue share for Showrunners before Legal clears it.
- A 20–30 Double season. The roster column already allows a higher cap. The constraint is cost and quality.
- Mid-run join during Survival.

An earlier draft that priced "always-on compute" as the subscription is not the plan. If a private town's cost later forces a meter, the meter stays inside the product.

## Sequence

**Through LeaderTalks night 15**

1. Run the season free. Leave the existing "Run Doubland for Your Group" door as it is. No second form. No price, preorder, tip, discount, or "Doubland for teams" line on the episodes or posts the cohort sees.
2. Operator log: night-1 people back on nights 2, 7, and 15; unprompted "that's so them" and asks for more; host-door submits that reach finished profiles. This log is the fundraising instrument.
3. Read this season's model cost per Double-day. Trailer render is extra and is not in that number.
4. Open a Stripe account and start Legal on team terms (consent, no employer access to quiz or private talk, deletion, no HR use, Showrunner cash question). No payment integration yet.

**At night 15**

5. Freeze the retention log.
6. Publish the team page: price, perks, privacy rules, up to 15 Doubles. The button is a short form to Ivan, not a card.
7. Five conversations at $2,500 with team leads outside the measured cohort. People inside the cohort only after their log row is closed. Listen for two things: they can repeat the privacy promise, and they accept a work season in which someone leaves.

**After two of those five ask how to pay**

8. Self-serve is the destination, not the test. Then build it: checkout creates a private town that sleeps at $0 compute; the card is saved and not charged; the charge happens when 5 profiles are complete; if the town is still asleep at 14 days, the card is released and nothing is captured. Do not authorize a card for 14 days (the hold expires). Do not charge now in order to refund later.
9. A purchase-order field is not how a company pays. First sales are a card or an invoice Ivan sends.
10. Door fork ("Friends or work team?") ships with that checkout. Friends still get a free town. Work goes to the paid town.

**When the nightly episode is shipping without an emergency**

11. Free friend town: sleeps at $0, wakes at 3 finished profiles, goes dormant after 14 days without quorum. Survival does not start a 15-night elimination under 6 profiles. Solos do not enter the featured cast.
12. Showrunner reward at 5 finished profiles is a credit on the episode. Personal cuts and gift packs wait until a personal cut has been rendered and timed.

**From group two**

13. Season Cut, as specified above. Rebooking a Team Season is the second company sale, offered at that team's finale.

## Kill lines

Judge at 60 days after the first real charge.

| Signal | Stop or change |
|---|---|
| Fewer than 2 Team Seasons paid at $2,000 or more | Drop the offer. Do not discount it to create a yes. |
| Team work delays a friend-group launch | Stop selling teams until the friend launch is out. |
| Delivered season cost (model + render) above about 15% of price | Raise the price or shrink the included cuts. |
| Season Cut under about 10% of spotlighted members | Fix the moment the offer names. Leave the price. |
| Of the first 10 real friend-group door submits, fewer than 3 reach 3 profiles in 14 days | Remove invite friction. Do not drop the quorum to 2. |
| Empty-town rate is the failure | Wake rate matters once public friend towns exist. During LeaderTalks the first metrics stay the cohort log. |

A Team Season that makes coworkers play safe will not renew. Treat that as a failed delivery, not as a marketing problem.

## Cost notes (do not put these in a deck)

These figures disagree with each other. Use a measured LeaderTalks Double-day, plus one timed personal cut, before a sales call or a seed number.

- June 2026 envelope: about $2–7 of model cost for one watched 15-Double day ($30–110 for a 15-night season).
- Later CTO note: about $0.051 per Double-day on the live model (about $12 for 15 people × 16 days), with per-Double cost rising as the cast grows.
- Other cited band: about $0.23–$1.39 per resident-day, which is $110–667 for a 30-Double season and can break the 15% line on a $2,000 sale.
- Included Season Cuts for every team member are render cost. They are not in the model figures.

`MAX_CONCURRENT_SIMS` defaults to 3 in code. Confirm the live value before promising overlapping seasons. `self_serve_max_roster` defaults to 15 and the setter allows up to 100. Selling 15 is a product choice.
