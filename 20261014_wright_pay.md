# Wright lens: what makes people pay (2026-10-14)
*Systems lens from Wright's public talks; not Wright. Quotes verbatim from KB quote files; rest paraphrased.*

## 1. Why people pay for something they value here
- **It's about them, not just footage.** A11 (~00:12–00:17) describes the pull of seeing an inferred version of yourself, and of comparing how friends saw the same events. A Season Cut sells *your* Double's arc. "Every player's path is unique because choices are the content." (A5_Quotes)
- **Agency beats spectating.** "Spectatorship hides what games actually deliver: agency and problem-solving." (A5_Quotes). Watchers who love a night pay more readily when the purchase gives them a stake: their Double, a gift, a vote.
- **Recognition is the currency.** "Recognition flows down, content flows up." (A3_Quotes). Spotlights and producer credit are what Showrunners and members value most.

## 2. Patterns that fit and are cheap to build
- **One-time, per-season unlocks, not subscriptions.** "Subscriptions throttle mass adoption more than design does." (A3_Quotes). This backs `plg_selfserve.md` §6: a $9–15 one-tap Cut right after the free spotlight.
- **Gifting.** Gifting a Cut to someone in the cast (§7) turns a purchase into recognition and a recruiting vector. "The contagious period has huge leverage on total reach." (A1_Quotes). Sell while sharing peaks.
- **A ladder, not a funnel.** "Communities are ladders—design for conversion, not just audience size." (A3_Quotes). The steps run viewer → spotlighted member → Cut buyer → Showrunner → team lead. The "Run a season for your team" finale footer (§4) is Plan B's cheap entry point,.
- **Team Season = sleeping town plus capture-on-wake** (§2). Zero buyer risk, little new build.

## 3. Making self-serve payment feel natural
- **Offer at the moment of recognition.** "Don’t make the computer tell a story; make it recognize one." (A1_Quotes). The offer should name the moment the sim detected ("your Double's betrayal on night 9"), never show a generic paywall.
- **Make it invisible and effortless.** "Great interfaces are invisible—lots of work you never notice." (A1_Quotes). Use a wallet button and no account wall. "Stories happen when publishing is effortless." (A1_Quotes). Sharing the cut should be one tap too.
- **No pressure mechanics.** Use no countdowns, never show who bought, and keep offers out of the group episode (§6, §8). "Players quickly sniff out how on-rails a world is." (A3_Quotes). They sniff out manipulative selling the same way, and it poisons trust in a product built on personal data (A11 ~00:28, consent and privacy).
- **Free first, always.** The free spotlight is the sample.

**Measure:** spotlight → share → Cut purchase, and gifts received → later buys. If the 10% Cut threshold (`plg_selfserve.md` kill check) misses, fix the moment-detection before touching price.
