### Why

The `importance_ele_n >= 10` bypass was added in `ffd7b9e1` (Aug 2025) as a safety net. In chat-heavy sims it becomes a runaway trigger: every utterance adds a memory item, the counter hits 10 within a few steps, reflection fires, counter resets, hits 10 again, repeat.

**Survival sim `20260406-3` documented the damage:** LLM call volume grew from **23 calls/step to 83 calls/step over 960 steps — a 6.1x throughput degradation**. Full report at `environment/frontend_server/storage/20260406-3_report.md`.

The fix restores the Stanford paper's original design: reflection fires only when the 150-point importance budget is depleted, so it's driven by **meaningful experience volume**, not raw message count. Nothing in your 90 commits depends on the bypass firing — your work is in conversation/location/pathing, none of which relies on reflection cadence.
