## Speed/timer recalibration + sprite walk fix (item 1)

**Goal:** the sim clock and the wall-clock must agree on the playback speed.

| Speed | Wall-clock per sim-minute (1 step) |
| --- | --- |
| 1× | 60 s |
| 6× | 10 s (default) |
| 15× | 4 s |

Formula: `ms_per_step = 60000 / speed`. One step = one sim-minute (`sec_per_step = 60`).

### What was wrong
The previous pacing base was `10000` regardless of speed, so 6× ran each sim-minute in ~2.5 s instead of 10 s (Ivan's report). Recalibrating to `60000 / speed` meant the **sprite walk now had to fill the whole step** (it used to last ~1–1.7 s with old tiles/s clamps). That change exposed two latent bugs.

### Single source of truth — `lib/playbackTiming.ts` (new)
Centralized all speed/timing constants (was scattered across ~8 files):
```
WALL_MS_PER_SIM_MINUTE = 60_000
stepDelayMs(speed)      = 60000 / speed          // replay loop pacing
walkDurationMs(speed)   = stepDelayMs(speed) * WALK_STEP_FILL
WALK_STEP_FILL          = 1                       // walk fills the step
LIVE_STEP_DRAIN_MS      = 10_000                  // LIVE drain cadence (BE-coupled)
LIVE_ANIM_SPEED         = 6                        // LIVE ≈ 6× wall-clock
SEC_PER_STEP            = 60
SLEEP_SKIP_SPEED        = 15
PLAYBACK_SPEED_OPTIONS  = [1, 6, 15] (default 6)
```
Consumers aligned: `usePlayback`, `useSmoothTimeline`, `useLivePlayback`, `AnimationManager`, `GameCanvas` (music), Redux default, skip-on-sleep, speed UI.

### Root cause #1 — clock mismatch (sprites froze at ~50% of their path)
**Symptom:** at 6× each sprite walked ~half its path (red debug line) then froze off-path for the rest of the step. Logs: `INTERRUPTED ... at t≈0.51`.

The safety timeout in `AnimationManager` was scheduled on Phaser's **scaled** clock while the walk tween ran on **wall-clock**:
- `scene.time.timeScale = speedMultiplier` (=6) — set in `MainScene` + `SpeechController`.
- Safety used `scene.time.delayedCall(15000, …)` → fired at `15000 / 6 ≈ 2500 ms` wall, then deferred `delayedCall(1000)` (≈167 ms wall each) × 12 max → force-stop at ~4.5 s.
- The walk tween (`scene.tweens`, `timeScale = 1`) lasted 9000 ms wall.
- `t = 4500 / 9000 ≈ 0.50` → exactly the observed `t≈0.51` kill.
- The force-stop did `t.stop(); t.destroy()` **outside** `killAllTweens`, so it never showed in the stack traces — that's why earlier hypotheses (next-step `killAllTweens` race, bad coordinates, multi-segment) were all dead ends. Ruled out with `[SPEED]` logs: `gapToPath0 = 0px` always, render paced at ~10 s, `activeTweens = 0` at each step boundary.

**Why it only appeared now:** the old walk (~1.7 s) finished before the ~4.5 s safety fired. Filling the step (9 s) made the walk outlive the safety → killed mid-path.

**Fix:** the safety timeout (and its deferrals + JS backup) now use wall-clock `setTimeout` (`safetyTimeoutWallId`, `safetyDeferralWallIds`), so it fires at the intended `max(expectedDurationMs * 1.5, 10000) ≈ 15 s` wall — a real fallback, no longer a mid-walk killer. Cancellation centralized in `clearSafetyTimers()`, called from `killAllTweens`, step-complete, force-stop, and shutdown. Steps with no real tweens (stationary/blocked) now resolve immediately instead of waiting the full safety window.

### Root cause #2 — ~1 s pause at the end of every step
After fix #1 sprites reached their destination but idled ~1 s before the next step. Cause: `WALK_STEP_FILL = 0.9` → walk 9000 ms vs pacing 10000 ms, and `usePlayback` does `Promise.all([animation, pacing])` (waits the max). **Fix:** `WALK_STEP_FILL = 1` → walk = full step (10000 ms at 6×), continuous step→step flow. Safe because the safety is now wall-clock and the loop never advances until the animation promise resolves.

### Left untouched (on purpose)
- `scene.time.timeScale = speedMultiplier` stays — still drives subtitle dwell / conversation stagger. Only the safety was wrongly coupled to it.
- Replay pacing (10 s/step at 6×) per Ivan's spec — not shortened.
- Fetch vs render decoupling intact — the fix was internal to `AnimationManager`, not the fetch loop.
