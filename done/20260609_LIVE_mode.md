## Experience (one sentence)

A viewer opens `doubland.ai/<sim>` and watches their household live the day **in sync with the owner's real-world clock**. The sim anchors at **06:30 owner-local**; at the live edge, **1 sim-minute = 1 wall minute**. The backend may generate ahead, but viewers are capped at **owner-local now** — scrub back freely, never into the not-yet-lived future. Playback opens at the day's first non-sleep activity, runs **6×** to catch up, then **1×** at the reveal edge. At **18:30 owner-local** a curated daily **Episode** drops; the Episode is the show, live Phaser is the always-on canvas.

**DP7 core rules (reference):**
1. Capture IANA `owner_timezone` at fork/finalize; anchor `start_date`/`curr_time` to **06:30 owner-local**.
2. At reveal edge: 1 sim-min = 1 wall-min; catch-up speeds only when viewer is **behind** `reveal_step`.
3. Reveal cap is **FE-only for MVP** — BE-5 hardening before monetisation/spoiler-sensitive audiences (`TODO_post_mvp.md`).

## Known tradeoffs (MVP)

- **Catch-up bubble pacing vs proximity zones (#1, 2026-06-15):** LIVE catch-up uses sub-linear bubble dwell (`bubbleTimeScale`: 6× playback → ~2× wall, 15× → ~4×) while sprites/steps stay linear. `SpeechController` 3-zone logic (DRIFTING queue cap, APART drop) keys off sprite distance — at 6× catch-up personas can hit APART before all enqueued lines display. **Not a data bug** (same step chat on scrub/replay 1×). Rationale: subtitle UX research (12–20 CPS band; incomplete reading at linear high speed). Full chat on 1× edge/replay; catch-up is intentionally lossy. Code: `double-front/lib/playbackTiming.ts`; post-MVP: scale zone thresholds or freeze zone-check during catch-up.
