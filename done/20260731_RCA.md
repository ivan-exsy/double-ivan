## 1. Goal (plain language)

Make Survival / free-flow **generation good enough for public MVP playback**:

1. **Bodies and places feel real** — Doubles go where the act implies (cafe seat for coffee, bathroom for bathroom, named venue when traveling there). Fake props (piano-as-lunch-table) and fake destinations (bathroom stretch → Park) stop being common.
2. **Talk feels real** — Doubles hold full conversations when they **want** to (and can **seek** each other when they do). Passing by still allows **brief hellos** with a sane cooldown — not cafe talk-loop spam.
3. **Keep hard world rules** — Privacy, staff-only, sleep/bed, restricted props. Prefer encoding those as **context the model may choose from** (curated options) plus a thin safety net — not as ever-growing “nearest arena” heuristics that invent semantics.
4. **Do not block MVP gen on creator Talk learning** — Chat-with-Double life-chapter apply (Talk Path A) can wait; it does not define in-sim realism.

**Non-goal this round:** Another surgical “patch the crutch” pass like `ivan/mvp-residual-pass` without changing who owns the decision.
