## 1. North star — what we are optimizing for

### Product promise
Doubland builds AI Doubles from **how real people actually communicate**, drops them into a shared world, and puts them under Survival pressure so **character shows**. Daily trailers exist to make strangers (and the in-group) excited enough to watch, share, and return.

### The recognition test (creative acceptance bar)
For any featured Double on a given day, at least one of these should land:

1. **Blind voice** — name stripped from a chat snippet; an acquaintance IDs them above chance.  
2. **Blind choice** — KEEP/RISK, alliance move, or vote has a one-line “because that’s how they are” link to their known traits.  
3. **Life texture** — job/place/habit shows up as *them living*, not only as a nameplate before strategy talk.  
4. **Share trigger** — someone who knows them would forward the clip with a reaction (“this is so X”).

### Why trailers failed the MVP bar on this package
`20260711-1` Day 2 has a **real episode shape** (Shield → social night → 9-vote boot) but **weak recognition**. Voices converge on the same spy-ops register. Downstream script/ranking cannot invent “so them” if the sim did not produce it.

## 2. How we ensure Double profiles capture real people

### 2.1 L-Talks path (current production cast)

This season’s 15 Doubles were **not** built from a self-serve quiz. Pipeline (opener / scenario brief):

1. Take ~**one year** of real alumni group chat (~300 members).  
2. Select the **15 most active** voices.  
3. Run a **message-history profiling** pass (proprietary) — personality, argument style, values, tone, what they push / reject — **as revealed in communication**, not self-report surveys.  
4. Write an evidence-tagged **soul** document per person (message IDs as citations).  
5. Import soul → database profile snippets + in-sim identity fields.  
6. Assign **personality-matched** in-world job + home (operator-approved binding).  
7. Press play (Survival season).

**Names on screen** are fictionalized for the in-group (recognizable to peers, not a public doxx). Photos are generic AI — not real faces. Portrayals must stay **affectionate / flattering**, not exposé (consent triage: brief senior/sensitive subjects first).

### 2.2 What a “soul” contains (creative review surface)

Template: `double-ivan/psy/soul.md`.  
Production examples: `generative_agents/souls/<persona-uuid>.md` (15 files).

High-signal sections creative partners should review:

| Section | What it gives the Double |
|---|---|
| Core stance / values | What they fight for and refuse |
| Interaction / voice fingerprint | How they sound (cadence, humor, directness) |
| Decision style | Fast vs slow, evidence vs intuition, conflict style |
| Motives / fears | What pressure should reveal |
| Do-not-do / hard nos | Guardrails against caricature |
| Calibration scenarios | “In situation X they usually…” |

**Example (Ivan Pitts soul — compressed):** sensemaking over slogans; rejects black-and-white framing; debate-forward; tests assumptions; results over performative politeness. Source: Leadertalks chat export with message citations.

### 2.3 Runtime fields that must stay true to the soul

| Field | Meaning | Feeds |
|---|---|---|
| `innate` | Core traits (from values) | Plans, chats, votes, reflections |
| `learned` | Decision heuristics | Same |
| `currently` | Present focus / role framing | Day context |
| `lifestyle` | Day brief (Survival rewrites mornings with challenge/vote deadlines + leftover life) | Planner |
| `living_area` / `work_area` | Home + job in the village | Movement, “who they are” stamps in video |
| **Snippets** | `speaking_style`, `values_and_principles`, `decision_heuristics`, `social_rules`, `topic_attractors`, `do_not_do` | Richer prompt context (when wired) |

**Creative ask:** For each of the 15, confirm the soul’s one-page snapshot still feels like the real person. Flag wrong / mean / off-voice lines before we invest in fidelity eng.

**Eng note:** Self-serve “create my Double” personality capture is **deferred**. Binding jobs/homes for *already-profiled* Doubles is the MVP path.

## 3. How personality is supposed to create believable behavior

### 3.1 Design thesis (Survival playbook)

> Survival converts high-fidelity personality simulation into **competitive social theater**.  
> **Pressure creates character** — scarcity, deadlines, elimination, and information asymmetry force people to reveal themselves.  
> Agents are **not puppeted**. Structural incentives + Identity Overlay; personality still produces the schedule and decisions.

Normal life (job, errands, cafe) should still exist in leftover hours. Survival bends *attention and talk*; it should not erase *who they are*.

### 3.2 Cognitive loop (simplified for directors)

Every Double, every few sim-minutes:

**Perceive → remember → plan → act / move → chat (if near someone) → reflect**

Survival turns on `survival_mode` and swaps in survival-aware prompts that inject an **Identity Overlay**: today’s challenge, deadlines, alliances, threats, who’s gone, rewards.

### 3.3 Where personality *should* show up

| Moment | Expected “so them” signal |
|---|---|
| Morning plan | How they prioritize challenge vs job vs people |
| Challenge decision (e.g. KEEP vs RISK) | Risk style matches soul (cautious vs bold) |
| Cafe chats | Voice fingerprint + values, not generic strategy slang |
| Vote | Target + reasoning consistent with heuristics / relationships |
| Farewell / night reflection | Their worldview in one line |

### 3.4 Gap vs today (honest)

Documented product risk (VC prep): Survival drama has often been **emergent from neutral starting states (0.5)** — not strongly personality-seeded.  

Observed on `20260711-1`: rich souls exist, but **day talk homogenized** into shared ops language. The strongest “so them” beat was often the **elimination farewell line**, not the day’s chats. That means pressure is producing *game players*, not *these people under pressure*.

**This is the root eng workstream.** Creative partners define the target reveals; eng makes the loop obey the soul.

## 4. Simulation framework — rules scenario directors must abide by

Think of the village + Survival season as a **constrained stage**, not a writer’s room that can dictate every line.

### 4.1 Season shape

| Clock | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Engine day 1** | Grace / premiere — Survival **dormant** (cast lives; no challenge/vote) |
| **Engine day 2** | **Survival Day 1** — first challenge + first elimination night |
| **Later engine days** | Survival Day 2, 3, … until last standing (open-ended default) |

Cast size this season: **15** → up to **14** elimination nights in a full run.

### 4.2 Daily phase loop (competitive days)

Rough beat sheet every Survival day:

1. **Morning** — day brief injected (challenge + vote deadlines); plans regenerate  
2. **Challenge window** — gather, decide, resolve rewards (e.g. Shield / immunity)  
3. **Social afternoon** — talk, seek allies, scheme (proximity-gated chats)  
4. **Voting evening** — secret ballot at gathering spot (default Hobbs Cafe)  
5. **Elimination** — one Double out (if not protected); farewell; survivors react  
6. **Night** — decay / clear; prepare next day  

Spatial rule of thumb: enough alive Doubles at the gathering location advances phases; timeouts exist so the day cannot stall forever.

### 4.3 Hard limits (do not assume you can)

| Limit | Why |
|---|---|
| **No line-by-line puppeting** | Doubles choose words via LLM + memory + personality; director sets *structure*, not dialogue |
| **No forced chat without proximity** | “Seek” can walk A toward B; talk still needs them to meet |
| **No skipping cognition** | Perceive/plan/reflect still run — Survival reshapes context, not the architecture |
| **Cannot invent physics minigames** | Challenges are **social / decision / information** games (dice, pacts, votes…) |
| **Cannot silently swap souls mid-season** | Profile changes need deliberate re-import / review |
| **Cannot treat false memories as canon** | Digests sometimes elevate wrong beliefs (“Owen’s out”) — quarantine for story |
| **Brand / consent** | Affectionate portrayal; in-group recognizability is intentional and sensitive |

### 4.4 Day indexing trap (for briefs and VO)

- “Eliminated on day 1” in season data = **Survival Day 1** (= engine day 2), **not** the premiere.  
- Trailer CLI `--day 2` = first competitive daily.

## 5. Tools on the scenario director’s board (entertainment levers)

You cannot write every line — you **can** shape the possibility space so entertaining, recognizable behavior is likely.

### 5.1 Structural levers (shipped / designed)

| Lever | What you control | Creative use |
|---|---|---|
| **Challenge pick / rotation** | Which game runs that day (catalog; default random without replacement) | Force different skills: risk, trust, signaling, coordination |
| **Challenge rules** | KEEP/RISK, tokens, pairs, etc. | Create dilemmas that match cast personalities |
| **Gathering place & deadlines** | Where/when challenge and vote happen | Public pressure cooker (cafe) vs scattered scheming |
| **Immunity / rewards** | Who is safe tonight | Reshape the vote board; create “untouchable” drama |
| **Vote & elimination** | Secret ballot; boot leaves the cast | Irreversible cost — episode climax |
| **Identity Overlay** | What every Double “knows” about today’s stakes | Shared pressure without identical *reactions* |
| **Social seek** | Soft “try to find X” movement intent | Increase chance of key conversations |
| **Season length posture** | Open-ended vs capped | Pacing of dread / endgame |
| **Grace day (Day 0)** | Première without boots | Habitat intros, relationships before blood |

### 5.2 Challenge catalog (palette)

Examples of available social games (full list in survival challenge SOT / playbook appendix):

- **Roll for the Shield** — private die; KEEP or RISK reroll; highest wins immunity; ties draw  
- Limited Immunity / tokens, paired dilemmas, team pressure days, bluff/call games, etc.

**Director craft:** Pick challenges that *reveal* different souls (e.g. truth-seeker vs calculator vs social broker), not only ones that maximize random chaos.

## 8. Investigation — personalization levers (founder ask + eng findings)

Founder hypotheses we investigated on `20260711-1` + code/SOT:

### 8.1 Roles + leftover daily routines (non-survival life under Survival)

**Design intent (already shipped):** Survival morning rewrite says challenge + vote are mandatory; **leftover hours should still feel like this person’s life** (job/hobby/errands). Baseline lifestyle is captured before rewrite. Soft seek can walk toward someone — chat still proximity-gated.

**What we observed on this day:**

| Evidence | Finding |
|---|---|
| Ivan’s `daily_plan` | **Real job blocks present** — pharmacy 7:00–10:30 and 13:00–17:00, lunch, dinner, then vote |
| Cast digest `schedule_summary` (all 15) | **Mixed:** every Double has some sleep/life lines **and** heavy “review challenge notes / strategy” blocks |
| `lifestyle` field at runtime | **Fully replaced** by a long Survival brief (baseline only appears as a parenthetical inside that brief) |
| Seek instruction in leftover hours | Explicitly asks them to find people to talk **strategy / alliances / tonight’s vote** — pushes ops talk into “life” time |

**Verdict:** Leftover-routine plumbing is **partially working** (jobs appear in plans). It is **not enough for “so them”** because (a) many hours still fill with generic challenge prep, and (b) social leftover time is steered toward the same strategy grammar. Recognition failed in **how they talk and choose**, not only in whether they clock into the pharmacy.

**Eng follow-ups:** Keep job blocks; stop “review challenge notes” filler; soften seek copy so leftover social time can be *their* topics (work gossip, values, humor) not only vote ops; optionally keep a short baseline lifestyle line visible in ISS alongside the survival brief.

### 8.2 Communication style + decision-making (primary recognition channel)

**Design:** Souls include speaking fingerprint + decision heuristics. Runtime snippets include `speaking_style`, `decision_heuristics`, `do_not_do`, etc. (`profile_context_builder`).

**What we observed:**

| Path | Personality depth today |
|---|---|
| Daily plan / hourly schedule prompts | Can pull profile snippets (wired) |
| Survival **chat** | Uses short ISS (`innate`/`learned`/…) — **snippet injector not wired into chat** |
| Survival **vote** | Asks “based on your personality…” but same threat/ally framing for everyone |
| Survival **challenge** (Roll for Shield) | Private die + **shared generic advice** (“A 5 or 6 is usually worth keeping…”) — actively flattens risk style |

**Verdict:** Profiles *exist* and *can* personalize behavior, but the **loudest Survival moments (chat / challenge / vote) barely use the rich soul**. Shared Overlay + shared challenge heuristics + strategy-seek copy dominate. This matches the creative team’s “hive grammar louder than souls” diagnosis.

### 8.3 Do profile settings allow personalized behavior, or need tuning?

**Two layers:**

1. **Soul content quality** — L-Talks souls are evidence-rich (message citations). Creative should still spot-check for caricature / missing tells (top-8 bible).  
2. **Wiring / weight** — Even perfect souls won’t shine if Survival prompts don’t load `speaking_style` / `do_not_do` and if Overlay injects the same register for all.

**Eng take:** Prefer **wiring + anti-clone** before mass soul rewrites. Spot-tune souls only where creative flags a wrong tell. If blind tests still fail after wiring, then deepen souls.

### 8.4 Do daily challenges reveal personality?

**Catalog:** Yes — dilemmas differ (HOLD/FOLD, Protect/Expose, lock alliances, public justification…). RealityTV’s proposed slate (`hold_for_shield` → `silent_pact` → `alliance_lock_in`) is **in the engine catalog** with dedicated prompts.

**This day’s challenge (Roll for Shield):** Good mechanical surprise (six-way tie → draw), **weak personality reveal** because:

- Heuristics in the prompt are one-size-fits-all.  
- Reasoning is 1–3 sentences with no requirement to sound like *them*.  
- Video teach card for `roll_for_shield` / `hold_for_shield` is **missing** from `build_fact_ledger._CHALLENGE_CARDS` → empty `brief` / `effect_plain` in this package.

**Verdict:** Challenges *can* reveal personality if decisions + reasons are soul-conditioned and the dilemma type matches the cast. Default random + generic heuristics will not.

## 9. Answers to Questions for Eng

### Q1. Pass/fail “this could be them” test before VO?

**Pass gate (instrument before share CTA / Remotion / locked VO):**

On the fork day’s **featured leads** (min 3):

1. **Blind voice** — strip names from 2 cafe/chat snippets per lead; ≥1 acquaintance (or founder proxy) IDs the person **above chance**, and snippets must not be ops-clone (“pillar/refill/hardware”).  
2. **Blind choice** — show KEEP/HOLD vs RISK/FOLD (or vote + one-line reason) without name; reviewer maps it to the soul’s decision tell (“they’d never risk a 4”).  
3. **At least one life-texture beat** in schedule or chat that is job/habitat-true without false social canon.

**Fail → no VO lock.** Human scratch VO for internal review only is allowed; public/share package waits.

We will encode this as a short checklist in the day package (`recognition_gate.md`) filled by founder/creative before `lock_day_script`.

### Q2. How do false facts (“Owen’s out”) get blocked from digests/ledgers?

**Policy:**

| Surface | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Fact ledger** | Only season-true elim / votes / challenge winners. Never chat claims. |
| **Digest Moments** | Demote or quarantine any moment that **contradicts** ledger (e.g. “X is out” when X ∉ eliminated). |
| **VO / scars / cliff pings** | Ledger-true only (matches engagement P3). |
| **Upstream (harder)** | Belief hygiene in reflection/memory so false elim beliefs don’t get poignancy ≥7. |

**Near-term eng:** contradiction filter at digest build time. **Next:** stop hallucinated boots hardening in cognition.
