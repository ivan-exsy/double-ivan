## 3. Specific clearance questions

### A. Primary brand `DOUBLAND`

1. **Availability in US (USPTO), EU (EUIPO), UK (UKIPO), Canada (CIPO)** in Classes 9, 38, 41, 42.
2. **Domain/social-handle conflicts** — we already hold `doubland.ai`; please confirm no infringing prior registrations.

4. **Registration recommendation** — file in which jurisdictions first, priority order, projected timeline + cost.

### B. Tagline `What if?`

5. **Is `What if?` registrable as a standalone tagline** in our classes, or is it too short/generic to merit standalone protection?
6. **Is it registrable as part of the composite mark `DOUBLAND — What if?`** (two-line lockup design mark)?
7. **Likelihood of confusion analysis vs. Marvel/Disney's *"What If…?"* mark:**
   - Marvel's mark is "What If…?" (with ellipsis) — registered in entertainment classes for an animated series
   - Ours would be "What if?" (with question mark, no ellipsis)
   - The marks are **semantically identical** but **typographically distinct**
   - The underlying products are **substantively different** (Marvel = animated multiverse anthology; Doubland = unscripted live AI sim of real people)
   - **Specific risk we want assessed:** does Disney's existing mark block our use, even with the typographic difference?

### C. Composite lockup `DOUBLAND / What if?`

8. **Can the two-line lockup be registered as a single design mark** (more defensible than two standalone word marks)?
