**Project:** Polished mobile-first (9:16) day-overview trailer for simulation Day 1

## Project Overview

We are producing a high-quality, mobile-first vertical trailer that recaps a full simulation day in a reality-TV style. The trailer follows a clear narrative arc (pressure → plan → countermove → reveal → new tension) with voiceover, scene footage, cast intros, and brand-consistent graphics.

This is the first of what we expect to be an ongoing series of day-overview trailers (and potentially other formats). If the collaboration works well, there will be regular work on future episodes and expanded scope.

1. **Primary deliverable**

   - Must feel production-level: clean pacing, balanced audio, crisp typography, consistent visual treatment.

2. **Reusable asset package** (delivered in a dedicated sub-folder)  
   - All graphics, overlays, lower-thirds, name cards, end-card elements, color grades, fonts, and any custom frames or placeholders created for this trailer.

3. **Production Handover Note** (short written document)  
   - A concise “how we built this” note covering:  
     - Tool chain and key settings used  
     - Typography and color decisions  
     - Pacing/transition approach  
     - Any custom techniques for scene handling or audio lock  
   - Written at the level a senior editor would need to reproduce the look and feel quickly for the next episode.  
   - This is standard practice on series work so the brand stays consistent — nothing more.

## Style & Creative Direction

- **Reference**: Latest rough cut (`trailer_16x9.mp4`) + the creative spec in `20260514_trailer_day_overview.md`.
- Key principles:
  - Reality-TV pacing with clear dramatic arc
  - First-name-only voiceover, full names on cards
  - Clean typography (no grey highlights)
  - Brand wordmark open + cohort end card
  - Concrete, observable storytelling — no invented emotion or motive
- We want a mobile-first vertical treatment that feels native on phones while preserving the cinematic quality of the source material.

I am happy to review 2–3 direction options at the midway point and pick the strongest path forward. Reviews are collaborative — your creative judgment on execution is valued.
