## Day Structure & Major Beats

The day followed a classic **pressure → plan → countermove → reveal → new imbalance** arc:

1. **Ivan** positioned himself as the planner, staking a back corner and recruiting Katya and Gosha into a voting pact built around a subtle nod signal.
2. **Katya** held the "cue sheet" role — she would only give the nod if she saw a loud defection, acting as the group's safety valve.
3. **Gosha** publicly reinforced the pact by repeatedly confirming it with both Ivan and Katya, raising the perceived cost of any betrayal.
4. At the vote, the bloc fractured. Ivan was eliminated despite the plan.
5. **Katya and Gosha** survived with strengthened positions; Ivan's organizing role is now gone.
