# Day-Overview Trailer — Creative Guidelines for Editor

**Purpose**  
Produce polished, mobile-first vertical trailers that recap a full simulation day in a reality-TV style. Each trailer tells a clear dramatic story with voiceover, scene footage, cast intros, and consistent brand treatment.

---

## Trailer Structure (Day 1)

**Total runtime target:** ~125–145 seconds

### Opening (Establishing Layer)
1. **Concept Reset Card** (first 8–10 seconds)  
   - Clean DOUBLAND wordmark still  
   - The line: *"They are not game characters. They are Doubles — AI versions of real people, making choices no one wrote for them."*  
   - Followed by one cast-intro card per main character (hero image + name + short intro line)

2. **Cast Intros**  
   - Realistic hero image backdrop  
   - Character name + one-line role descriptor (e.g., "Protected planner", "Signal-keeper")

### Main Arc (the day story)
Follow this emotional beat structure:

**today_pressure → apparent_plan → countermove → vote_reveal → new_imbalance**

- Every beat opens with a full first name.
- Use concrete, observable actions only — no invented motives or emotions.
- End the trailer on a question, fact, or rising tension.

### End Card
- Brand end card showing cohort name + "DAY N ENDS" + watch CTA + cadence line.
- Must start only after the final voiceover line finishes (tail guard).

---

## Voiceover & Narration Rules

- One continuous voiceover for the whole day (not separate lines per scene).
- Use **first names only** in the voiceover.
- Full names appear on cards and subtitles.
- Keep language natural and conversational.
- Connect beats with clear narrative tissue ("but", "by nightfall", "what none of them knew").

---

## Visual Treatment

- **Opening title:** Clean wordmark still (no motion push-in for day recaps).
- **Arc scenes:** Phaser footage inside a gold card border + AI-written caption (lower center) + character sketch portrait in alternating top corner with name tag.
- **Typography:** Clean, no grey highlighting on any text overlays.
- **Pacing:** Reality-TV style — clear dramatic arc with rising stakes.

---

## Brand Discipline (Strict)

**Forbidden words** (except the one allowed trailer line above):  
simulate, agent, AI twin, AI version, digital copy, game character (outside the opening reset line)

**Tone:**  
Concrete and observable. Name who did what, to whom, where. Never invent emotion or motive. Never reference step numbers or internal IDs.

**Doubland pronunciation:** two syllables ("double" + "land").
