## Writer's note (draft)

The reveal lands at ~20s: scale (300) → method (messages, not surveys) → cast size (15) → action (pressed play). The cast block is visual-only — `group_anim` lets all fifteen land at once without burning runtime on intros. Survival is named once at the close, not in the body. The mid-URL and end card double-anchor `doubland.ai` for cold viewers who scrub or watch silent.
