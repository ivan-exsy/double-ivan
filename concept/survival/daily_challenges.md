Below are **15 challenge ideas designed for your current constraints**: text-based reasoning, social interaction, memory, voting, alliances, limited tokens.
No physics, no real-time skill, no visuals required. All are **pressure-first** and cheap to implement.

I’ve grouped them by *what social muscle they stress*.

---

## I. Scarcity & Tradeoff Challenges

### 1. **Limited Immunity**

* Only **2 immunity tokens** exist.
* Doubles must decide who gets them (self, ally, trade, promise).
  **Forces:** negotiation, favoritism, betrayal.

---

### 2. **Energy Rationing**

* Only X players can act at full strength tomorrow.
* Others get vote penalties.
  **Forces:** sacrifice vs self-preservation.

---

### 3. **Shared Survival Pool**

* Pool of resources shared by the group.
* Any Double can secretly take more.
* Pool affects next challenge difficulty.
  **Forces:** trust erosion, tragedy of the commons.

---

## II. Alliance & Coalition Stress Tests

### 4. **Self-Selected Teams**

* Doubles must form teams for safety.
* Last team formed = automatic risk.
  **Forces:** social exclusion, hierarchy emergence.

---

### 5. **Leader Nomination**

* Group must nominate a leader.
* Leader gets immunity but must assign penalties.
  **Forces:** power abuse, scapegoating.

---

### 6. **Alliance Lock-In**

* Alliances formed today are binding for 2 votes.
* Breaking causes public penalty.
  **Forces:** commitment anxiety, fake loyalty.

---

## III. Prisoner-Dilemma Variants

### 7. **Silent Pact**

* Pairs choose: *Protect* or *Expose*.
* Outcomes asymmetric and hidden.
  **Forces:** betrayal patterns, retaliation loops.

---

### 8. **Group Betrayal Game**

* Group votes secretly to cooperate or defect.
* One defector gains power if others cooperate.
  **Forces:** paranoia, trust collapse.

---

## IV. Information Asymmetry Challenges

### 9. **Secret Intel Drop**

* 3 Doubles receive true info (e.g. voting history).
* 2 receive false info.
  **Forces:** misinformation spread, credibility tests.

---

### 10. **Whisper Chain**

* Message passed Double-to-Double, distorted each time.
* Final version revealed publicly.
  **Forces:** manipulation, lying, reputation damage.

---

### 11. **Anonymous Accusation**

* Each Double submits one anonymous accusation.
* Accusations partially revealed.
  **Forces:** witch hunts, fear signaling.

---

## V. Reputation & Public Pressure

### 12. **Public Justification**

* Votes are public.
* Each vote requires explanation.
  **Forces:** face-saving, moral posturing, hypocrisy.

---

### 13. **Reputation Tax**

* Most disliked Double loses vote power.
* Least disliked gains influence.
  **Forces:** image management, fake kindness.

---

### 14. **Trial Night**

* One Double put “on trial.”
* Others argue for or against them.
* Outcome affects immunity.
  **Forces:** persuasion, coalition rhetoric.

---

## VI. Endgame / Chaos Injectors

### 15. **Mutually Assured Destruction**

* One Double can sacrifice themselves to eliminate another.
* Choice is secret until revealed.
  **Forces:** revenge, martyrdom, fear escalation.

---
