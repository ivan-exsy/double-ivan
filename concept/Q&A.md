# Doubland — Spoken Q&A

**Use:** Memorise. Say this out loud. Do not read it as a deck.  
**Status:** Internal draft (2026-08-25). Not ready-to-send investor copy.  
**Locked from founder interview:** one hook for every room · friend groups first · **adults first (Telegram MVP); teens later, not the intro** · virtual life is already winning, then the playground · show, then waitlist · right person, any seat · magnetism, not dollars.

**How to use**

- Lead with the hook. Then name a friend group. Then one proof (a trailer, not a metric).
- Answers are 2–4 sentences. Stop. Let them ask the next question.
- Tags: `[all]` everyone · `[vc]` only if they are investing · `[customer]` group / team / brand · `[join]` co-founder or teammate · `[push]` if they keep pressing · `[later]` only if they drag teens / schools / COPPA into the room.
- Current vs Desired: if it is not live tonight, say so. Do not sell the far corner of the plane as shipped.
- Never say Matrix, BMI, brain-computer, or Neuralink. Plant the feeling. Let them name the destination.
- **Audience:** primary room is adults. MVP is a Telegram group of adults. Teens may join later. Do not introduce Doubland as a kids product.

**Pronounce** Doubland as *Dohb-land* (like *double*, never *doubt*). Doubles live **in** Doubland (a place), not *on* it.

## What it is

### What is Doubland? `[all]`

Doubland is where “What if?” gets answered — by your Double, living the version of your life you didn’t. Your friend group gets a private village. The Doubles live on their own. You watch. The daily trailer is the story you cannot stop sending to the group chat.

### What is a Double? `[all]`

A Double is your personality twin — not a costume, not a chatbot. Built from who you actually are. In about fifteen seconds you should think: “That’s me. I would have done that.”

### Is this a game, a social network, or a show? `[all]`

It is a show your group is inside of. The village is the backlot. The trailer is the episode. You do not scroll strangers. You do not grind a game. You watch people you know, including you.

### Who is it for? `[all]`

Start with a friend group of adults. A squad that already has inside jokes — right now, a Telegram group we can actually run. Same spine for teams, alumni, brands that want to exist as places. Teens may join later. They are not the primary audience, and they are not the intro.

### Why friend groups first? `[all]`

The magic is recognition plus people you already care about. A stranger village is a cartoon. Your village is gossip you are allowed to love.

## Why it exists

### Why are you building this? `[all]`

I wanted a tool for my son. A safe place to test a bold choice, hear a wiser read, then do it in real life. I do not want him to grow up only inside a feed. I want the hours he already spends on a screen to make him braver offline.

The company in front of you starts with adults, because that is the group we can run honestly today.

### Isn’t this just escape? The Matrix? `[all]`

Virtual is already winning. People are not going back to a world without phones. The open question is what those hours are *for*.

Doubland is a safe playground. Hard conversations and life choices get tested here, then implemented out there. If it ever feels “better,” it is because you had more room to become — not because we asked you to abandon the real world.

`[push]` I am not selling a replacement planet. I am selling a scratchpad with a soul.

If they reach for a famous science-fiction word: pause. “Now you see where the plane goes.” Do not confirm the word. Do not put it on a slide.

### What is the emotional promise? `[all]`

You see yourself clearly. You try the scary thing at zero real-world cost. Then you walk into the date, the hard talk, the thing you keep putting off — already having lived a version of it.

### Why now? `[all]`

Three things landed at once. Generative AI can finally act like a person you recognize. Short video trained everyone to feel a full arc in sixty seconds. And virtual life already has the hours — we can turn those hours into rehearsal instead of empty scroll.

The window to name this category is open. It will not stay empty.

## How it works

### What do I actually do in Doubland? `[all]`

You create a Double. You join a village with real friends. You watch them live. You follow anyone. You replay the moment. At the end of the day you watch the trailer. At night you can whisper a little guidance while your Double sleeps.

Live line, if you need it: “Watch live 24/7. Follow any Double. Replay every moment.”

### What are the trailers? `[all]`

The trailer is the product people feel. One minute. Your day, or the group’s day, cut like a tiny episode. That is what you send. That is what makes someone else want a Double.

### Can I control my Double? `[all]`

Not like a puppet. That would kill the show. You steer gently — dream-chats when it sleeps, a goal, a “try this tomorrow.” The Double still has to live it. Recognition dies if you joystick every step.

`[push]` Over years we go deeper on control. Today the point is a twin you recognize, not an avatar you drive.

### What is Survival / RealityTV? `[all]`

Same village, more pressure. Daily challenges. Votes. Alliances. Someone goes home. Character shows up when it costs something. That is the format we are running as the first public-feeling season.

### What are Social Experiments? `[all]` `[customer]`

A guided season for a team. Same Doubles, a designed pressure (the new person, the leaked message, the one VIP invite). You watch what the group actually does. Then you talk about it for real — with the sting already used up in the village.

`[later]` Schools can run the same spine later. Do not lead with classrooms.

## Try it / customers

### Can I try it tonight? `[all]`

Yes — I can show you. Watch a trailer with me, or watch Doubland live. Then request an early invite at doubland.ai. It is not a self-serve “make my village in five minutes” product tonight.

If they are in the Telegram MVP: say we are running it with that group. Do not sell a public app store download.

### I have a group. Can you build one for us? `[customer]` `[vc]`

That is the sentence we want. Yes — tell me the group. Request “bring this to my group” on the site, or talk to me directly. The first seasons are invite-only on purpose. Density beats a big empty world.

### Who pays? `[customer]`

Individuals start free enough to fall in love with the show. Groups and organizations that want their own season are the first people with a checkbook. Brands can exist in the village the way they exist in real life — as places, not ads in a feed.

Do not quote prices unless you have locked them. If they ask: “We will price a season with you. The site is the waitlist, not a checkout.”

`[later]` Schools and SEL packages are not the current checkbook.

### Will this be safe for real people? `[all]`

Villages are invite-only. The Double is a mirror for the group, not a public psych profile. We do not sell someone’s inner life. If a portrayal is wrong or unwanted, it comes down. Consent, claim, remove — that is the line.

`[push]` Do not claim “fully compliant,” “COPPA certified,” or any legal guarantee counsel has not given. Say: invite-only, claim/remove, no public dossiers, we take this as a hard constraint.

### Will my kid be safe? `[later]` especially parents

Only if they bring kids. Then: villages stay invite-only. We do not sell a child’s inner life. Teens are not the primary audience and not the MVP. If we ever invite them, safety is a hard constraint, not a growth lever.

`[push]` Still do not claim “COPPA certified.”

### What if the Double is mean, or too accurate? `[all]`

If it is a little too true, that is the product — in private, with people who already know you. If it is cruel, or someone wants out, we pull it. Delicious truth. Not an ambush.

## Investors

Default: **no dollars, no MAU, no ARR.** Proof is magnetism. Use this section only in a VC room, and only if they ask.

### Why is this a company, not a toy? `[vc]`

Reality TV proved people will watch humans under pressure for twenty years. It cannot scale: one cast, huge cost, months of production. We invert it. Every friend group is a cast. Every day is an episode. Compute is the crew. That is a media format, not a feature.

### What is the raise proof today? `[vc]`

Not vanity volume. A dense adult group watching Doubles of people they know — and then asking, unsolicited, “build one for my group.” Quotes and inbound beat a 15-person user count. If we do not have those quotes yet, say that. Do not invent intensity.

The Telegram MVP is the first room. It is not a TAM slide.

### Are you raising? How much? `[vc]` only if they ask

Founder-funded to date. No outside round closed. If they ask the number: we are lining up a seed on the order of five and a half million to finish the live village, the daily show, and the first group seasons — after the magnetism is real enough to put on a slide. If they do not ask, do not volunteer the number.

### How do you make money? `[vc]` `[customer]`

The format monetizes like media, not like a generic SaaS checkbox.

- Groups pay for a season.
- People pay to make the daily story beautiful.
- Brands pay to exist as real places in the village.
- Teams pay for guided experiments.

Say the menu. Do not say a twelve-month ARR target. Those figures in old narrative docs are a plan, not a fact.

`[later]` Schools can be a later checkbook. Do not put minors in the monetization intro.

### What’s the moat? `[vc]`

Time plus recognition. The longer you live with your Double, the more it is *you* — memory, relationships, the “that’s so them” that a fresh GPT chat cannot fake. The village graph of a real group is not something a competitor copies on Tuesday.

If they say “this is just GPT”: “GPT talks. A Double lives with your friends for days and still sounds like you. That reaction is the tech demo.”

### Why you? `[vc]` `[join]`

I have been building the village, the Doubles, and the daily show as a founder-funded company — not a slide. I know the format because I am making the episodes. The personal reason is my son. The company reason is I will not stop until the playground exists.

### What’s the risk that kills you? `[vc]`

Two honest ones. The Double is not recognizable — then it is a cartoon and nobody cares. Or we get recognition and get sloppy on consent — then we do not deserve to exist. Everything else is execution.

`[later]` If they ask about teens: expanding to minors without earning that trust would also kill us. That is why teens are not the intro.

### What does the seed actually buy? `[vc]` only if they ask

A product people can feel every day: live villages, daily trailers, light steering, the first RealityTV and group seasons. Deeper worlds and deeper control come after the habit is real. I will not spend the round on a Matrix demo that nobody watches.

If you are obsessed with this mission and can carry a whole side of the company, we talk. The seat is not pre-locked — builder, format, product, growth. I am not handing out a title to someone who thinks this is a cute AI wrapper.

Because the category is still unnamed and the work is already on the table: a living village, a daily show, a mission I will not put down. You would not be joining a pitch. You would be joining a world that is already running.

### What do contractors vs co-founders mean here? `[join]`

I already work with specialists — picture, front end, craft. A co-founder is different. They own an entire side. They stay when it is ugly. We find that out by working, not by a coffee.

## Comparisons (say these cold)

### Is this The Sims? `[all]`

The Sims is a dollhouse of strangers you puppet. Doubland is a show about you and your real friends. You watch more than you drive.

### Is this TikTok / a feed? `[all]`

TikTok makes you the audience of everyone else. Here you are the star of a story that is actually about you — and the people you already sit with.

### Is this Character.AI / a chatbot? `[all]`

A chatbot talks to you. A Double lives with your friends when you are not looking. The point is what it *does*, not what it replies.

### Is this Black Mirror? `[all]`

Black Mirror is the warning. We are building the positive version: rehearsal, not a trap. If it only makes people more stuck in a screen, we failed.

### Is this a metaverse / second life? `[all]`

Those asked you to move in. We ask you to practice, watch, and come back kinder and braver. Place, not platform — but the point is still this life, improved.

## Hard questions

### This feels creepy. You are profiling people. `[all]`

A mirror in a private group is not a dossier on the internet. We use personality so the Double is *you*, not a random NPC. We do not publish a psyche. We do not sell the inner file. If that line ever blurs, we are in the wrong business.

### What about consent if you make Doubles of real people? `[all]` `[vc]`

People can claim their Double or ask to be removed. We fictionalize names for outsiders. Inside a group, people will recognize each other — that is the point, and it has to be affectionate, not an exposé. If someone is not okay, they are out.

### Isn’t this dangerous for lonely people? `[all]`

Lonely people already live in feeds that do not know them. A private village with real friends, plus a path back to the offline choice, is the opposite of a parasocial hole. If someone has no group, we do not fake them a crowd and call it love.

### Isn’t this dangerous for lonely teens? `[later]`

Only if they bring the old story. Then: teens are not the primary audience. If we invite them later, a private village with real friends is still the opposite of a parasocial hole — and we do not fake a crowd for a kid who has no group.

### You have no traction. `[vc]`

Correct if you mean millions of users — we are not pretending. What we are proving is pull: will a hard-to-impress adult group watch, forward, and ask for their own season? That is the slide. If that slide is not ready, I will not fake it.

### What is live vs vapor? `[all]`

Live: Doubles in a village you can watch, daily trailers, an invite waitlist, Survival as a real mode. The first real room is an adult Telegram group. Not live: tap the app and spin up any group tonight; a world you cannot tell from film; being inside the life instead of watching it. I will show you the live thing. I will not sell you the horizon as a ship date.

If there is a whiteboard: draw two axes. Across — what you see (today’s pixels → cinema). Up — how deep you are (night whisper → being there). Leave the top-right unlabeled.

### What’s next? `[all]`

Make the daily show undeniable. Get groups asking for their own village. Then go deeper — richer worlds, more agency — only if people already cannot look away.
