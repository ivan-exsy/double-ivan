# Doubland Brand Reference — "What if?" System & Voice

**Date:** 2026-05-12 | **Use:** Asset creation (trailers, UI, marketing, onboarding, press)

---

## Positioning (one sentence)
**Doubland is where the question "what if?" gets answered — by a Double, your personality twin, living the version of your life you didn't.**

Doubles = answers. Cohorts = experiments. Viewers = people with their own "what if?" watching others get answered.  
**Rule:** Every asset must either *pose* the question or *answer* it. If it does neither, cut it.

---

## The Name "Doubland"

Two readings the name should evoke — both intended, both equally valid:

1. **Double + Land** — the land where Doubles live. A place, not a platform; a populated world, not a service.
2. **Dublin echo** — a real-sounding capital city. Doubland is the bridge between virtual life and real life, just as Dublin is a bridge between two worlds (old / new, Gaelic / Anglo, island / continent). The familiar phonetic shape ("Dublin"-adjacent) makes it land as somewhere people *live*, not a tech product.

**Prepositions matter.** Doubles live **in** Doubland (a place), never **inside** Doubland (a container). "On Doubland" reads as a platform — also wrong. The only correct frame is the geographic one: *in Doubland, from Doubland, across Doubland.*

**Pronunciation.** "Dohb-land" — same first syllable as *double*, never *doubt*. TTS guidance maintained in `video/showrunner.py` (`TTS_PRONUNCIATION_OVERRIDES`).

---

## What a Double Is (core concept)
A **Double** is a digital twin — a faithful snapshot of your real personality, voice, decision patterns, and social instincts. It is not a generic AI character or invented persona. It is you, unfiltered. Branding must make viewers feel instant recognition: "That's me — I would have said/done exactly that."

Recognition lives in **actions, conversations, and life choices**. Every asset must deliver the "that's my Double" moment within the first 15 seconds.

---

## The "What if?" System — Flexible Contexts
"What if?" is not a fixed slogan. It is a **steering question** whose power comes from the completion.

- **Register A — Open Question** (brand): Naked "What if?" — wordmark, homepage hero, trailer end cards. Viewer fills in. Pure invitation.
- **Register B — Completed Question** (format): "What if [specific]?" — The ending is context-appropriate and can draw from fear, desire, regret, hope, curiosity, or any personal moment. A precise completion steers the viewer's attention and curiosity toward the exact emotional hook of that cohort, episode, or social asset. E.g. "What if you said it?" (fear of speaking up) / "What if you stayed?" (regret of leaving) / "What if you took the job?" (ambition vs. safety).
- **Register C — Answer** (product): "Your Double did." / "Find out." / "You already lived it." — CTAs, reveals, response copy.

The three registers should *never appear together in one asset*. They alternate across surfaces. This creates the franchise feeling.

---

## Core Emotional Value (the payload — use everywhere)
Second-person only. These four pillars compress the mission into repeatable lines.

1. **Recognition** — "Your Double is a twin of your personality. You recognize your own voice, pauses, and patterns in every action, conversation, and choice. In 15 seconds, you know it's yours."
2. **Rehearsal** — "Try the hard conversation, the big ask, the scary choice—while real stakes stay zero. Your Double takes the risk first."
3. **Bravery** — "Do the thing you always wanted but were afraid of. Watch what happens. Decide if you want it IRL."
4. **Mirror** — "Every 'What if?' you never asked gets lived out. Then you see the version of you that said yes."

**Signature phrase:** "The version of you that..."

## Asset-Specific Rules (copy/paste ready)

**Video Trailers (v2.2+)**
- 0–4s: Pure A — bare "What if?" over dusk village + cyan wireframe. No explanatory VO.
- Cold open / tease: B only (one context-steered question per major character).
- Mid beats: Double does the feared thing → real-person recognition cut (Pillar 1).
- End card: Bare "What if?" (A) on screen. Let VO carry the closing hook ("The game is on."). The end-card text carries the daily cadence ("Episode 1 tomorrow · 18:30"). Remove all "Who will stay alive?" danger text from card.

**UI / Onboarding / In-Game**
- Commission flow: Single field "What if ____?" (B seed). Auto-suggest from pillars.
- Character cards / status: "This Double is rehearsing the version of you that speaks up."
- Night reflection prompts: "Your Double chose courage here. Would you?"
- Forbidden: "simulate," "agent," "AI twin," "digital twin," "virtual you." Always "your Double," "your personality twin," or "the version of you."

**Marketing & Social**
- 0–90 days: Only A + pillar sentences in long-form (About, press kit, bios). No B variants yet.
- 90+ days: Cohort launches lead with B (context-steered); episode recaps use B → C. Brand moments return to A.
- Shareable viewer cards: B question + "Your Double answered it." (C) + wordmark (A).

**Press / Decks / About**
Use canonical sentence above, then one pillar. Never lead with mechanics.

## Vocabulary Discipline
**Retire (internal + external):** Imagine, try/experiment (verb), alternate reality/parallel life, digital twin/AI version/virtual you, simulate/agent, "What if!! 😊" (rhetorical).

**Promote:** Answer/answered, run the question, your question, "the version of you that...", "You already lived it.", "Before you have to.", "personality twin", "your voice / patterns / choices in action".

---

## Visual & Tone Guardrails
- Question mark = brand element (defined size/kerning to wordmark; may stand alone as logomark).
- No italics on "What if?" — keeps it real, not rhetorical.
- Cyan wireframe never touches wordmark or tagline (different planes).
- Every Double in promo must be paired with a Register B question and read as a real-person twin (never generic AI).
- Tone test: Would a real person say this seriously to themselves at 11pm? If no, rewrite.

---

**Success test for any asset:** Does it make the viewer complete their own "What if ____?" in their head *and* instantly recognize their Double as their personality twin? If yes, it's on-brand.
