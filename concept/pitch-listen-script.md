**Use:** Play the MP3. Do not read this in a room. The spoken lines are what your mouth should own.  
**Audio:** `concept/audio/pitch-listen.mp3` (opener `warm`: eleven_v3 @ 1.2×)  
**Status:** Internal. Not a sendable investor tape.

| Plane | Low (today) | High (they infer) |

## Part 1 — Manifesto (~4–5 min)

Listen until this feels like something you already believe.

---

People say they want real life.

They say it while their thumbs are already somewhere else.

The hours have moved.

Friendship happens in a chat.

Courage happens later. Or never.

Virtual is already winning.

That used to scare me.

Then I saw the opening.

If the hours are already there — we still get to decide what those hours are for.

I started this as a tool for my son.

A place he can take a hard moment… and live a version of it… before he has to live it for real.

Not a lecture.

A playground.

You and your friends put personality twins into a private village.

They live.

They laugh. They choke. They fall for someone. They say the thing you were afraid to say.

You watch.

And every day you get a one-minute story of what happened.

That story is not about strangers on a feed.

It is about you.

In fifteen seconds you should think: that is me. I would have done that.

Then, at night, you whisper to it.

A little guidance. A little courage.

Then you walk into the cafeteria. And you do it.

This is not another app.

This is a new kind of show.

Every person in the group is the star.

People will ask me where this goes.

I do not give them a science-fiction word.

I give them a plane.

If there is a whiteboard, I draw it.

Across the bottom: what you see.

Today it is simple. Pixel people.

You watch, and your mind does the work.

You already know how to turn a sketch into a face.

You have been doing that since the first cartoon you loved.

We are walking that line toward a world you cannot tell from film.

Up the side: how deep you are in it.

Today your Double behaves like you.

At night you talk to it, while it sleeps, and you send it into tomorrow.

That is watching. That is whispering.

The top of that line is not more buttons.

The top is being there.

The day the line between watching a life… and living it… disappears.

I do not name that destination.

I let them feel it.

If they say the word everyone says — I let the room have it.

They connected the dots.

I stay on the next honest step.

Because the point was never to run away.

The point is a safe place to become.

Watch. Learn. Try the bold thing.

Then do it in the real world.

If you have a group — I want to build you a season.

If this is the thing you cannot stop thinking about — we should talk.

Want to see what your Double would do?

## Part 2 — Room lines (~3 min)

A voice will name the room. Then you hear the words. Steal the cadence, not a recitation.

---

**The hook. Every room.**

Doubland is where “What if?” gets answered — by your Double, living the version of your life you didn’t.

You and your friends put personality twins in a private village. They live. You watch. Every day you get a one-minute story of what happened.

**Try it tonight.**

Let me show you. Watch this with me. Then request an early invite at doubland.ai.

It is not a make-my-village-in-five-minutes product tonight. The first seasons are invite-only on purpose. Density beats a big empty world.

**Someone has a group.**

That is the sentence I want. Yes. Tell me the group. I want to build you a season.

**Someone wants to join.**

If you are obsessed with this, and you can carry a whole side of the company — we talk.

The seat is not pre-locked. I am not handing out a title for a cute wrapper.

**A VC. Money.**

I do not lead with a number.

The proof is magnetism. A hard room watches people they know… and then asks, unsolicited: build one for my group.

Quotes beat a user count.

If they ask what I am raising: founder-funded to date. Seed when the pull is real enough to put on a slide. I do not invent intensity.

**Whiteboard. Only if there is one.**

Two lines.

What you see — from today’s pixels, where your mind fills in the life… toward a world you cannot tell from film.

How deep you are — from a twin you recognize, that you guide at night… toward the day you are not watching a life. You are in it.

I do not write a word in the top-right corner.

I let them write it.

**If they say that word.**

Pause.

Now you see where the plane goes.

I will not put that word on a slide.

I will put the next honest square on the map.

**Close. Pick one and stop.**

Want to see what your Double would do?

If you have a group, I want to build you a season.

Watch this one-minute story. Then tell me you don’t want one.

I don’t need you to believe a slide. I need you to feel the pull — then decide.
