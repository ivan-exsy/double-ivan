## Category

**Doubland is a private show about you and your friends.** Your Doubles live in a shared village. You watch. You practice who you could become — without the real-world cost.

Same category, investor wording: *the first scalable media format in which every user is the star of their own reality show.*

---

## Why I want to build Double

I want to build Double because I believe the greatest gift we can give humanity is a safe mirror that shows us who we could become.

Right now, billions of people—especially teenagers—navigate the most important years of their lives without a rehearsal stage. They make irreversible social mistakes, carry unspoken fears, and never get to see the version of themselves that chooses courage, kindness, or wisdom. Double changes that.

It creates a living digital twin of you—built from a simple quiz, then set free in villages with your real friends. 
There, your Double lives autonomously: it laughs, argues, falls in love, fails, and triumphs in real time. 
You watch. You learn. You whisper “dream-chats” at night to steer it gently toward the choices you wish you could make in real life.

This is not escape. This is preparation.

Every rehearsal, every trailer you share, every “Did you see what my Double did?” moment becomes a quiet revolution in self-awareness and empathy. 
Over time, the line between simulation and reality blurs: 
- the person who practiced kindness in the village starts showing up kinder in the cafeteria;
- The one who rehearsed asking someone out finally does it
because they already lived the best possible version of that moment.

I want to build Double because I want every young person to have a second chance at becoming their best self—without the pain of public failure. 
I want to turn the terrifying, lonely process of growing up into something joyful, shared, and profoundly human.

## What makes Double so appealing and great

Double is not another app. It is a new category of experience: **personal mythology made playable.**

It feels like the love child of *The Sims*, *Black Mirror*, and the best summer camp you ever had — except the characters are real people you already know.

- **The magic of watching yourself** — Your Double moves through the village with your personality, your quirks, your secret hopes. Seeing it navigate drama, romance, or conflict is like watching a private Truman Show where you are both the star and the audience. The 1-minute trailers it generates every day become addictive little movies of your own life.
- **The power of shared worlds** — When your squad drops into the same village, something electric happens. Inside jokes form in minutes. Alliances and betrayals play out in real time. The RealityTV mode turns it into *Survivor* with your actual friends—complete with daily challenges, secret votes, and dramatic eliminations. Social Experiments let schools and teams run safe, guided simulations of real-world pressure.
- **The safety of rehearsal** — Before you confront a friend, ask someone out, or make a big decision, you can test every version of the conversation in the village. Your Double tries Quick Win, Future-You, and Most-You paths. You watch the outcomes. You learn. Then you walk into real life armed with wisdom you earned without the cost.
- **The beauty of reflection** — Every night your Double sleeps and you get to reflect. The system surfaces insights: “Today you chose kindness when it was hard.” “Your Double avoided conflict—maybe that’s a pattern worth exploring.” It turns entertainment into gentle, private therapy.

That is why great people will be drawn to this mission.

We are not building another social network. We are building a **positive simulation layer for humanity**—a place where the next generation can practice love, leadership, courage, and connection until those qualities become second nature in the real world.

Double is the ultimate act of optimism: the belief that if we give people a beautiful, safe, joyful place to become their best selves, they will.

This is the greater purpose.

Join us. Let’s build the mirror that helps humanity see—and become—its highest self.

## Who it’s for

**Gift / long-horizon:** people — who already live in social networks and games, and want a low-pressure way to explore identity and group dynamics.

**What we introduce today:** adults, in invite-only groups we can actually run. Teens may come later. They are not the spoken intro. Safety for minors stays a hard constraint.

---

## Product shape

**Default:** invite-only **Live Village** — Doubles act autonomously; users watch; light steering when the Double sleeps (dream-chats); daily/shareable trailers as the feeling engine.

**Modes on the same spine (not separate products):**

| Mode | Job |
|------|-----|
| RealityTV / Survival | Character under pressure — challenges, votes, coalitions |
| Social Experiments | Guided group dynamics / SEL-style rehearsal |
| (Future) Research on Doubles | Aggregated, privacy-preserving insights — **not** a near-term priority |

The simulation is the studio backlot. **Edited feeling (trailers)** is the primary expansion and sharing engine.

## Long-horizon destination

Not a ship checklist. Direction of travel. In a room, sketch the plane only if a whiteboard exists; leave the far corner unlabeled ([`vc_msg.md`](vc_msg.md) §Spoken room locks).

| Axis | From → toward |
|------|----------------|
| **Believability (what you see)** | Abstract 2D → richer 3D → fully believable worlds |
| **User control (how deep you are)** | Personality snapshot + autonomous play → goal/direction via chat → deeper immersion (eventual BCI-scale control is speculative, not a near-term commitment) |

As believability and agency grow, Doubland remains a **positive simulation layer** — preparation and rehearsal, not escape. The day the line between watching a life and living it disappears is the destination. Do not treat that day as a ship date.

## Non-goals

- Not another generic social network or feed.
- Not a productivity / “AI agent that acts in the real world by default.”
- Not dark-pattern teen acquisition or public psychographic exploitation.
- Not treating Draft forks (e.g. `v1_opensource/`) as locked product doctrine until the founder decides.
