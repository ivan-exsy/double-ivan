# Doubland — Investor Narrative

---

## The One-Sentence Hook

**Doubland is the first scalable media format in which every user is the star of their own reality show.**

We do not build another social network or game. We are inventing a new category of entertainment: personalized, user-generated reality that produces daily, shareable 60-second stories user want to watch and share.

---

## The Media Format Opportunity

Reality TV has dominated attention for two decades because humans are wired to watch other humans under pressure. But traditional reality is expensive, slow, and limited to a few dozen participants per season.

Doubland flips the model:

- Every participant is the protagonist.
- Every day generates a watchable “day-in-life” trailer.
- Every friend group becomes its own private reality series.
- The content is *infinitely* personalized - replayable - shareable.

This is not “The Sims meets TikTok.” This is a new native format — the first reality medium that scales to millions of simultaneous casts while remaining intimate to each viewer.

---

## The Retention Engine (Why Users Cannot Look Away)

The product is designed for daily habit and emotional addiction:

1. **Instant recognition** — Within 15 seconds a user sees their Double make a choice they recognize as their own. The “that’s literally me” moment is the core hook.
2. **Daily trailers** — Every simulation day produces a 1-minute highlight reel that feels like a miniature Truman Show episode. Users check the app to watch what their Double did.
3. **Dream-chats & gentle steering** — At night users whisper guidance to their sleeping Double. The Double incorporates those learnings the next day. This creates agency without breaking immersion.
4. **Squad FOMO & social proof** — When your real friends are in the same village, the drama is personal. Sharing trailers becomes the new group chat.
5. **Cliffhangers & streaks** — Notifications like “Tensions rising at the café” and daily check-in rewards keep users returning.

The loop is self-reinforcing: watch → reflect → steer → share → repeat. Early data shows users returning multiple times per day once they see their first trailer.

## The Path to Matrix — Our 2D Strategic Roadmap

We are not building a game that gets prettier. We are building a simulation layer that eventually becomes preferable to real life for social rehearsal, identity play, and emotional experience.

We track progress on two explicit axes:

### Y-Axis: Realism of Visualization
- Today: 2D Phaser sprites with status overlays, speech bubbles, and subtitles.
- Near-term: 3D environments with cinematic lighting, dynamic cameras, and micro-expressions.
- Long-term: Photorealistic or hyper-real rendering where the visual fidelity is indistinguishable from captured footage.

The goal on this axis is to make the simulation look and feel like a high-end movie or live reality show rather than a game.

### X-Axis: User Immersion & Participation Abstraction
- **Current state (lower end):** Users recognize themselves in their Double’s autonomous choices. They can only steer indirectly through “dream-chats” when the Double is asleep. The Double then folds those learnings into the next day’s behavior.
- **Future state (higher end):** Users can assume direct, moment-to-moment control of their Double and live inside the simulation as a true second life — with full sensory, emotional, and decision fidelity.

The upper-right quadrant of this graph is the Matrix end-state: a simulation so believable and controllable that it becomes the preferred place to practice high-stakes social moments, explore alternate identities, and experience emotional arcs that are richer, safer, and more repeatable than real life.

This is not escapism. This is rehearsal at the highest fidelity. When the simulation feels better than reality for the things that matter most — connection, courage, belonging — users will spend increasing portions of their emotional lives inside it.

## Business Model & Moat

- **Freemium core:** Free village creation, basic playback, and daily trailers. Premium unlocks deeper dream-chat steering, ad-free experience, advanced controls, and custom village rules.
- **School & brand experiments:** Social Experiments mode becomes a paid offering for educators and organizations running guided simulations (SEL, leadership, conflict resolution, market research).
- **Data moat:** The longer a user plays, the higher-fidelity their Double becomes. This creates a proprietary behavioral graph that improves personalization and enables aggregated insights for research customers.
- **Platform flywheel:** More users → richer villages → better trailers → higher retention and sharing → more users. The 2D roadmap accelerates this flywheel by making the experience progressively more addictive.

## Monetization Streams

Doubland monetizes natively as a new media format. Every premium feature either deepens immersion for users or lets brands participate inside the reality show. The four primary streams are designed to offset the core infra cost ($2.6M annual at 100k MAU) while creating multiple high-margin revenue lines.

### 1. Branded Simulation Real Estate (Primary for Seed)
Brands pay to place their locations inside active villages (Giant Eagle, Starbucks, Carnegie Library, local high-school hangouts, etc.). These become natural backdrops where Doubles work, date, study, or hang out — exactly as in real life.

- Pricing: $8k–$25k per location per 6-month placement (tiered by brand size and exclusivity).
- 12-month target: 8–12 paid locations generating **$180k–$250k ARR**.
- Why it works: Teens already accept branded environments in games and social platforms. Done tastefully, it increases world realism and gives brands measurable engagement inside a new reality format. This stream directly offsets infra cost and validates the media thesis.

### 2. Premium Day-in-Life Trailers
Users pay for personalized, high-production 60-second trailers focused on their own Double (cinematic editing, voiceover, music, emphasis on key emotional moments).

- Pricing: $0.99–$1.49 per sim day (or $9.99/month unlimited for heavy users).
- 12-month target: 12–15% of DAU convert → **$220k–$280k ARR**.
- Why it works: The trailer is the emotional payoff. Users who love watching their Double will pay to get a polished, shareable version instead of the raw replay. High willingness-to-pay because it turns personal simulation into personal media.

### 3. Premium Simulation Creation & Ownership
Power users (individuals or small groups) can spin up private, longer-running, or custom-rule simulations with advanced controls, longer memory windows, and priority compute.

- Pricing: $4.99–$6.99 per sim day (or $29.99/month for unlimited personal villages).
- Target users: Heavy individual users + corporate/educational buyers running private experiments.
- 12-month target: 3–5% of MAU + early corporate pilots → **$90k–$130k ARR**.
- Why it works: Gives superfans and organizations ownership of the simulation experience. Corporate buyers (leadership training, market research, team-building) pay premium rates for private, branded environments.

### 4. Branded Social Experiments & Insights Licensing
Organizations pay for guided, measurable simulations (SEL programs, leadership challenges, product concept testing) plus access to aggregated, anonymized behavioral insights from the participant cohort.

- Pricing: $15k–$40k per multi-week experiment + $8k–$15k for insights package.
- 12-month target: 15–25 paid experiments → **$280k–$380k ARR**.
- Why it works: Schools, nonprofits, and brands already run expensive focus groups and SEL programs. Doubland delivers safer, repeatable, data-rich versions at lower cost. This is high-margin, high-credibility revenue that also seeds organic user growth.

**Combined 12-month revenue target from the four streams: $770k–$1.04M ARR** — comfortably covering the $2.6M infra run-rate at scale while leaving clear margin for the Series A.
