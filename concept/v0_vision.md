## **Overview**

*Double - Intro*
Double is an AI-powered social simulation app that lets users create a digital twin—called a "Double"—of themselves through a quick personality quiz, then drop it into shared virtual worlds with real-life friends or groups. These avatars interact autonomously in fun, unpredictable scenarios like peer drama or triumphs, delivering binge-worthy entertainment while sparking self-reflection and stronger group bonds.

### *Options*

#### **Primary. Live Village (Default)**
Friends join a shared (invite-only) virtual village and have fun watching their Doubles interact live—chaos, drama, and wins unfolding in real time. Users can steer their Double indirectly via “dream-chats” when the Double is asleep, including asking it to do something on their behalf (e.g., “Ask Jenny if she wants to be my girlfriend”) and rehearsing high-profile decisions in a stress- and risk-free environment before doing it IRL.

#### **A. RealityTV**
Villages can run in a **RealityTV** mode (think *Survival*): a simulation director sets daily challenges all Doubles must go through, and each night the group gathers to vote someone out—until one Double remains for recognition (and potentially prizes). 
This fork is designed to surface character under pressure and let coalitions, social strategy, and recognizable behavior patterns emerge naturally.


#### **B. Social Experiments**
Villages can also run as **Social Experiments**: guided simulations that stress-test group dynamics (leadership, conformity, conflict, inclusion) under controlled prompts, then turn what happened into shareable highlights + “what actually worked” takeaways. This creates value for high school squads, clubs, and team captains (and counselors/educators running safe SEL-style exercises) who want a low-stakes way to rehearse messy social moments before they blow up IRL. Example: “one VIP invite + a misunderstood DM + a new kid joining the group” becomes a mini episode you can binge and discuss—like a personalized, private social-psych reality show you can rerun with different rules.

#### **C. Marketing Research (Interview Doubles)**
As users spend more time with their Double—chatting, steering scenarios, and reflecting—the system steadily enriches their profile so the Double becomes a higher-fidelity digital avatar of their preferences and decision patterns. In this fork, organizations can run market research by “interviewing” Doubles instead of pulling real people into surveys or focus groups, capturing fast feedback on products, services, or policies at scale. Individual profiles stay private, while research customers receive **aggregated, cumulative insights** (e.g., “perspective of 10,000 users ages 14–18 with X/Y psychological profiles”) with stats and segment breakdowns—think Nielsen-style panels, but powered by opt-in digital twins that keep getting smarter the longer people play.

## Key Features
- **Avatar Creation & Solo Rehearsals**: Kick off with a fun 25-question personality quiz to build your Digital Twin. Test solo dilemmas via "Double It!"—input scenarios (e.g., "Confront my bestie?") for personalized options (Quick Win, Future-You, Most-You) with timelines to preview outcomes.
- **Virtual Villages & Group Chaos**: Join or create closed villages for your IRL group. Avatars interact autonomously based on profiles, sparking unpredictable stories—watch the drama unfold in real-time sims with random events.
- **Engagement Hooks**: The primary virality vehicle is a family of shareable 1-minute trailers: daily “day-in-life” stories for your own Double, simulation-day highlight reels for the group, and multi-day drama trailers that compress the most resonant arcs into a bigger wow moment. Streaks, reflections, and anonymous snippets keep you checking in; premium "dream-chats" let you whisper ideas to your Double at night for indirect influence.
- **Controls & Themes**: Village masters add events (e.g., "zombie outbreak") or rules (e.g., survival votes) for replayability—balancing randomness with light steering for authentic thrills.

## Playback Engagement
- **Pronunciato (Over-Sprite Status Bar):** Each visible Double carries a compact floating status bar with its name plus 1–2 icons that summarize its current state at a glance. This helps viewers quickly understand who is doing what without opening any menus.
- **Sprite Detail Card:** Clicking a status bar opens a detailed character card for a quick deep-dive into the Double. The card surfaces identity, current sub-action, personality, location, time, learned background, current activity, schedule, memory, chats, and plan so viewers can understand not just *what* is happening, but *why*.
- **Action Subtitles / Scene Commentary:** The playback layer can show subtitles that summarize the most important visible moment on screen (e.g., “Gosha and Katya are chatting in the Ville”), with an auto-generated comment explaining the scene in simple, watchable language.
- **Video-Style Playback Controls:** The simulation is framed like watchable media rather than a traditional game, with familiar controls such as play, pause, rewind, and scrubbing so viewers can revisit moments and follow the unfolding plot more easily.

### Playback Engagement — Trailer Formats
The primary expansion and sharing engine is not raw simulation footage, but tightly packaged **1-minute trailers** built from simulation playback.

- **Day-in-Life Trailer:** A daily story told from the perspective of one specific Double, especially the user’s own avatar. This format should feel like a miniature Truman Show episode: the user is the main character, the narrator frames the day, and the trailer turns ordinary decisions into a watchable personal arc.
- **Simulation-Day Highlights:** A group-level recap of the most interesting, emotionally resonant, or socially important moments from a single simulation day. This is designed for all participants in that simulation, helping everyone quickly catch up on what mattered and who drove the day’s plot.
- **Simulation Drama Trailer:** A bigger, more cinematic recap that pulls from several simulation days and several Doubles to tell one especially captivating story. This format is designed for peak wow effect, stronger emotional payoff, and the kind of shareable social drama that can travel beyond the immediate participants.


## *Target Audience*
Double targets high school teens aged 13-18 in the U.S., a demographic eager for fun, low-pressure ways to explore social dynamics, personal identity, and group interactions.
This customer segment includes individuals who enjoy social networks & games, and potentially seeking subtle self-growth through insights into peer behaviors and decision-making.


## User Value
- **Epic Entertainment**: Like bingeing personalized TikTok chaos—spot your squad's quirks in hilarious sims, turning "What if?" into can't-miss daily drops that slay boredom.
- **Sneaky Growth**: Build self-awareness by seeing your Double's choices; turn insights into real-life wins with reflection prompts and "Level-Up Plays"—boosting confidence without the lectures.
- **Squad Bonding**: Sparks IRL convos ("Did you see our Doubles beef?"), normalizes teen pressures, serves like private, risk-free exploration.
- **Safe & Addictive**: No-judgment space with ironclad privacy—users obsess over the mix of fun and foresight, making Double the app you can't quit.

## Monetization & Growth
- **Freemium Vibes**: Free basics (quiz, villages, summaries); premium unlocks dream-chats, ad-free, and master controls.
- **Viral Strategy**: Easy video shares + group invites fuel organic growth; partner with schools/teams for custom villages. Aim for high DAU via notifications and squad challenges.
- **Metrics**: Track video shares, village joins, and "smarter choices" feedback to iterate.
