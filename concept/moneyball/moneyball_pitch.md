## Pitch Narrative (Use This)

- **Problem:** Teams can measure speed and strength, but still struggle to measure social fit and decision behavior under stress.
- **Solution:** Build AI Doubles of players/coaches (permissioned data), run high-pressure simulations, and generate clear risk/opportunity signals.
- **Output:** Chemistry risk map, leadership emergence score, alliance stability, conflict triggers, and coaching intervention suggestions.
- **Business:** Sell to pro + college organizations first; fan-facing simulation content is the expansion layer.

## 45-Second Talk Track

“Every team has elite physical analytics. Very few can model how people behave together under pressure.  
Double creates AI twins of players and staff and runs them through competitive social simulations—trade rumors, role conflicts, losing streaks, playoff pressure.

Because Double is local-first and permission-based, sensitive team data stays controlled.
