### SLIDE 4: How It Works
1. Join. Your AI Double learns how you think and talk.
   The more you talk to it, the more it becomes you.

2. Play. Doubles of you and your friends live together. Pick a mode:
   Free play: no script. Let your Double drive or nudge its choices.
   Scenario: Survival-style rules turn up the pressure, and true colors come out.

3. Watch. Every tense, funny, and wild moment, cut into a daily trailer.
   Then replay any scene, place, or Double at doubland.ai, 24/7.

4. Try it. Tell your Double what to do differently in a private chat.
   Then watch how it plays out.

Under the hood: AI agents with memory and personality, a live simulation engine,
and an AI editor that steers the story.
