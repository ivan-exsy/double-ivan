# Doubland — North star

Durable long-horizon vision. Not eng SOT. Distilled from mission + early vision; polish with founder sign-off.

---

## One-sentence category

**Doubland is personal mythology made playable** — a simulation-media product where every user is the star of their own shareable reality show, rehearsing who they could become with real friends in a safe shared world.

Alternate investor hook (same category): *the first scalable media format in which every user is the star of their own reality show* (`vc_msg.md`).

---

## Why it exists

Teens navigate high-stakes social years without a rehearsal stage. Doubland creates a living digital twin (“Double”) in shared villages with real friends — watch, learn, gently steer via dream-chats — so courage, kindness, and connection can be practiced without the cost of public failure. Entertainment first; self-awareness and empathy as the lasting gift (`mission.md`).

---

## Who it’s for

Primary: **US teens 13–18** who already live in social networks and games, and want low-pressure ways to explore identity and group dynamics (`v0_vision.md`).

---

## Product shape (Canon baseline)

**Default:** invite-only **Live Village** — Doubles act autonomously; users watch; light steering when the Double sleeps (dream-chats); daily/shareable trailers as the feeling engine.

**Modes on the same spine (not separate products):**

| Mode | Job |
|------|-----|
| RealityTV / Survival | Character under pressure — challenges, votes, coalitions |
| Social Experiments | Guided group dynamics / SEL-style rehearsal |
| (Future) Research on Doubles | Aggregated, privacy-preserving insights — **not** near-term Canon priority |

The simulation is the studio backlot; **edited feeling (trailers)** is the primary expansion and sharing engine (`v0_vision.md`).

---

## Long-horizon destination — Matrix axes

Not a ship checklist. Direction of travel:

| Axis | From → toward |
|------|----------------|
| **Believability (X)** | Abstract 2D → richer 3D → fully believable worlds |
| **User control (Y)** | Personality snapshot + autonomous play → goal/direction via chat → deeper immersion (eventual BCI-scale control is speculative horizon, not a near-term commitment) |

As believability and agency grow, Doubland remains a **positive simulation layer** — preparation and rehearsal, not escape (`mission.md`).

---

## Non-goals (Canon)

- Not another generic social network or feed.
- Not a productivity / “AI agent that acts in the real world by default.”
- Not dark-pattern teen acquisition or public psychographic exploitation.
- Not treating Draft forks (e.g. `v1_opensource/`) as locked product doctrine until the founder decides.
