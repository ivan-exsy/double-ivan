# Doubland — Product pillars

Features and bets should clearly serve one or more of these pillars. Use with [`alignment-rubric.md`](alignment-rubric.md).

---

## P1 — Double as self-mirror

The Double is a recognizable digital twin (quiz → personality → autonomous behavior). Users get “that’s literally me” recognition and private reflection — not a generic NPC.

## P2 — Shared world with real friends

Invite-only villages / squads. Drama and bonding matter because the cast maps to IRL people. Cold stranger-only worlds are secondary unless they serve cold-start without breaking intimacy.

## P3 — Watchable media loop

Daily and multi-day **trailers** (and playback as media) turn simulation into bingeable personal mythology. Share (“Did you see what my Double did?”) is a first-class job, not an afterthought.

## P4 — Safe rehearsal & light steering

Users influence without puppeting: dream-chats, gentle guidance, optional “Double It!” style rehearsals. The product is preparation and play — not real-world agent autonomy by default.

## P5 — Character under pressure (format modes)

RealityTV / Survival and Social Experiments surface alliances, courage, and social strategy under rules — still on the same Double + village spine.

## P6 — Privacy & teen safety

Ironclad privacy defaults; no-judgment space; minors’ data and psychographic use stay constrained. Growth tactics that prey on teens fail this pillar.

## P7 — Believability & agency over time (Matrix)

Investments that deepen world believability or meaningful user agency are on-mission **when** they still serve P1–P5. Pure tech demos with no mirror / share / rehearsal value are off-pillar.

---

## How pillars relate to Draft forks

`v1_opensource/` (local-first, world marketplace, etc.) may accelerate **scaling** (P7 / distribution). It is **undecided**. Until chosen, do not demote P1–P6 to fit that fork; score both paths with the alignment rubric and flag the open decision.
