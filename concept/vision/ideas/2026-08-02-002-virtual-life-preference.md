# Idea — Why-now: people already prefer virtual life

## Raw (founder)

People say they want real life, but more and more they find themselves enjoying their virtual lives more and more (e.g. kids are not playing outside, they prefer spending time communicating with friends in virtual environment — on their phones, in games, social networks).
