## 1. Metadata Description — Softening Needed

**Current (too aggressive):**
> Your Double is a personality twin living the version of your life you didn't. Solo rehearsals are live. Villages are invite-only in beta.

**Proposed softened versions (pick one or refine):**
- "Meet your personality twin in Doubland. Solo rehearsals are live. Villages roll out in beta, invite-only."
- "Your Double is a personality twin that explores the choices you didn't make. Solo rehearsals are live. Villages are invite-only in beta."
- "See what the version of you that said yes would do — in Doubland. Solo rehearsals are live. Villages are invite-only in beta."

**Recommendation:** Go with the middle option. It keeps the core promise but softens the "living the version of your life you didn't" claim.

## 3. Expert Summaries (Consolidated)

### Reality-TV Group (Burnett + de Mol + Parsons)
- **H1:** "Enter Doubland with friends."
- **Metadata Title:** "Doubland — Watch the version of you that said yes"
- **Rationale:** H1 sets the scene (place + friends); support line carries emotional hook. Avoids Double + Doubland repetition. "With friends" is non-negotiable because the product loop depends on social pressure among friends' Doubles.
- **CTA to test:** "Enter your Doubland"
- **Test goal:** 5 first-time viewers should understand (unprompted) that a version of themselves goes into a social place with friends.

### Will Wright
- **H1:** "Enter Doubland with your friends."
- **Metadata Title:** "Doubland — Where Your 'What If?' Gets Answered"
- **Rationale:** Clean separation of load. H1 = place + friends; support line = Double + personality + alternate choice. Prefers "Enter" over "Drop into" because it feels like crossing into a real place.

### Social Engagement Group
- **H1:** "Enter Doubland with friends."
- **Metadata Title:** "Doubland — What If, With Friends"
- **Rationale:** Short, clear, spatial, social. Avoids longer explanatory H1s that lose brand feel. Title is brandable and emotionally aligned.

**Consensus across all groups:**
- Support line is locked.
- Avoid "Double + Doubland" in the same sentence.
- "Enter" is the preferred verb.
- "With friends" must stay.
